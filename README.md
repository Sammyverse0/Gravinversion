# INVERZIO

> **A fast-paced endless runner where you control gravity itself.**

**INVERZIO** is a gravity-flipping endless runner set in a vibrant cosmic world. Dodge obstacles, switch between the floor and ceiling, collect coins, and survive for as long as possible while the difficulty continuously increases.

The game revolves around one simple but challenging mechanic: **flip gravity at the right moment to avoid obstacles and keep running.**

---

## 🎮 Game Overview

In INVERZIO, you are constantly moving through an endless cosmic track filled with obstacles and hazards.

Your primary ability is **Gravity Flip**. With a single input, you can switch between running on the floor and running on the ceiling.

As you progress:

* The track becomes increasingly challenging.
* New obstacle patterns and level chunks are introduced.
* You can collect coins to increase your score.
* Your gravity ability has a cooldown, making every flip an important decision.
* Procedurally generated sections make each run different.

### The Goal

**Survive as long as possible and achieve the highest score you can.**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌀 Gravity Flip Mechanic

Instantly switch gravity to move between the floor and ceiling.

Use gravity strategically to:

* Avoid obstacles
* Navigate narrow paths
* Reach safe routes
* React to unexpected hazards

### ♾️ Endless Procedural Generation

The game continuously generates track sections as you play.

This means:

* Runs can continue indefinitely.
* The track does not follow a fixed level layout.
* Different level chunks can appear during different runs.
* Every attempt can provide a different challenge.

### 📈 Dynamic Difficulty

The challenge increases as your score rises.

More difficult level chunks are gradually introduced, requiring faster reactions and better use of the gravity-flip mechanic.

### 🪙 Coin Collection

Collect coins while navigating the track and aim for a new high score.

Coins encourage players to take more challenging routes instead of simply playing it safe.

### 🔊 Audio Settings

INVERZIO includes an audio settings system with a volume slider.

The selected volume setting is persistent, allowing players to customize their audio experience.

### ⏸️ Pause System

Need a break?

The game includes a functional pause menu that allows you to pause and resume your run.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action         | Keyboard  |
| -------------- | --------- |
| Move Left      | `A` / `←` |
| Move Right     | `D` / `→` |
| Flip Gravity   | `F`       |
| Pause / Resume | `ESC`     |

## 🧠 Gameplay Strategy

Gravity flipping is more than just a movement ability — **timing is everything**.

### Tips

* Don't flip gravity too early when approaching an obstacle.
* Keep an eye on the upcoming path.
* Remember that gravity flip has a cooldown.
* Use the ceiling to avoid obstacles that block the floor.
* Collect coins when doing so doesn't put your run at unnecessary risk.
* As the difficulty increases, prioritize survival over risky movement.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

INVERZIO is designed around a simple principle:

> **Easy to understand, difficult to master.**

The basic gameplay can be understood almost immediately:

**Move → Dodge → Flip Gravity → Survive**

However, the combination of procedural generation, increasing difficulty, obstacle patterns, and the gravity cooldown creates depth that rewards practice.
